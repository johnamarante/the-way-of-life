The Way of Life: Privacy Policy

Effective October 28, 2026

The Way of Life is a Catholic catechism board game for Android and the web. This policy covers the Android app (com.thewayoflife.rosary) and the browser version at johnamarante.github.io/the-way-of-life.

What we collect

Nothing. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information. The Android app does not have permission to use the internet.

What stays on your device

Your saved game, your options and the parts of the Codex you have opened are stored only on your own device (in the app's private storage on Android, or in your browser's local storage on the web). They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app, or clearing the site's data in your browser, deletes them.

Links

Some teaching pages in the game link to outside websites, such as the Catechism of the Catholic Church. Those links open in your browser, and those websites have their own privacy policies.

Children

The game is made for young people and families. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
