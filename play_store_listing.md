**Full description:**

The Way of Life is a Catholic board game for young people and families. Your path is a rosary that climbs a mountain, from the waters of Baptism to the Cross, with the Blessed Virgin Mary guiding the way.

Every bead is a step:

- Catechism questions: true or false and multiple choice, with the answer and the teaching behind it. Turn on advanced questions from the Catechism of the Catholic Church when you are ready.
- Saint Challenges: read the clues and name the saint before time runs out. More than 200 saints, each with a portrait, from the Apostles to the Fatima children. Win a saint's card and use it to help you on the way.
- Prayer Challenges: put the lines of a prayer in order, in English or Latin. Drag them, or tap two lines to swap them. Miss one and you see the prayer in the right order, so you learn it either way.
- The Cross: a moral dilemma from everyday life. Choose well and you win. Choose wrong and you return to the waters of Baptism, go to confession, and climb again.

The Codex collects the prayers, saints, questions and dilemmas you have met, so you can read them any time. Parents can read every dilemma for all ages before their children play.

Options let you set the text size, easy prayers for younger players, advanced questions, lesser-known saints, and dilemmas for ages 13 and up.

No ads. No accounts. No data collected. Your progress stays on your device.
