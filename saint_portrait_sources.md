# Saint Portrait Sources

One image per saint in `Catholic_Saints_Trivia.csv`, from Wikimedia Commons, where every file has a free license, except ten you supplied yourself (Matthew, Mark, Luke, Leo the Great, Monica, William of Gellone, Louis IX, Carlo Acutis, Gregory the Great, Ambrose; marked "Supplied by you"). Most are Wikipedia's lead image for the saint; the rest were picked by hand where Wikipedia had no free image or the face was too small at card size.

**How to use:** open the link, click **Download** (or pick a size under "Other resolutions"), crop the face to 256 x 256, and save it as JPEG in the folder you keep portraits in, with the name in the "Save as" column. That name matches the `Portrait` column in the CSV.

**Credit needed:** public domain, CC0, and "copyrighted free use" images need nothing. The 14 images marked **Yes** are under Creative Commons licenses (CC BY or CC BY-SA): if you share the game, credit the author and license (an About or Credits screen is enough; the file page shows the exact credit line). CC BY-SA also means your cropped portrait is shared under the same license.

Sizes and licenses were read from Commons on 2026-10-26. The Commons images were downloaded with `tools/PortraitFetcher` (the ten supplied images were converted with its `--convert` option, and it skips their rows, so `--force` never overwrites them). All 113 were reviewed as 256 x 256 thumbnails. Acacius, Pancras, and Juan Diego still show a small face; no closer free image was found.
