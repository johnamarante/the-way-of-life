# Saint Portrait Sources

One image per saint in `Catholic_Saints_Trivia.csv`, from Wikimedia Commons, where every file has a free license, except twelve you supplied yourself (Matthew, Mark, Luke, Leo the Great, Monica, William of Gellone, Louis IX, Carlo Acutis, Gregory the Great, Ambrose, Macrina the Elder, Peter To Rot; marked "Supplied by you"). Most are Wikipedia's lead image for the saint; the rest were picked by hand where Wikipedia had no free image or the face was too small at card size.

**How to use:** open the link, click **Download** (or pick a size under "Other resolutions"), crop the face to 256 x 256, and save it as JPEG in the folder you keep portraits in, with the name in the "Save as" column. That name matches the `Portrait` column in the CSV.

**Credit needed:** public domain, CC0, and "copyrighted free use" images need nothing. The 25 images marked **Yes** are under Creative Commons licenses (CC BY or CC BY-SA): if you share the game, credit the author and license (an About or Credits screen is enough; the file page shows the exact credit line). CC BY-SA also means your cropped portrait is shared under the same license.

Sizes and licenses were read from Commons on 2026-10-26 (rows 1 to 113) and 2026-10-28 (rows 114 to 193). The Commons images were downloaded with `tools/PortraitFetcher` (the supplied images were converted with its `--convert` option, and it skips their rows, so `--force` never overwrites them). All 193 were reviewed as 256 x 256 thumbnails. For the 80 added on 2026-10-28, Wikipedia's lead image was replaced where it was not suitable for children (Judith with the severed head; nude figures of Adam, Abel and Job; the Witch of Endor scene for Samuel) or showed the saint too small (group scenes, a seal). Acacius, Pancras, and Juan Diego still show a small face; no closer free image was found.
